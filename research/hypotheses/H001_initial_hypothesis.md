# H001 ? The Invisible Player

## Question

Can observable market variables help infer hidden market states such as liquidity, volatility regime, participation, and behavior changes?

## Framing

This is a research hypothesis, not a trading claim. The goal is to determine whether latent or hidden states can be detected from observable features and whether transitions between states contain statistically meaningful information about future behavior.

## Data

- Broad-market ETFs and equity index proxies
- Volume and liquidity indicators
- Volatility measures
- Cross-sectional market participation signals

## Tests

- Inspect for regime shifts using rolling statistics
- Evaluate whether state transitions predict later realized outcomes
- Test for statistical significance and robustness
- Validate out-of-sample as a final step

## Decision rule

Proceed only if the effect survives validation and falsification, not before.
